# Western IT/AI for frontline and field workforce, safety, logistics and fleets (manufacturing, mining, oil & gas services, energy, agri/grain, food), 2024-2026

Source quality note for the report writer: almost every outcome number below is from vendor case studies (vendor site, casestudies.com mirrors of vendor material, vendor press releases) or third-party pricing aggregators. Independent or analyst-verified outcome data was very rare. Many case studies are undated; where a date was visible it is noted. Treat all effects as "vendor-reported".

## 1. Connected worker / frontline apps (Augmentir, Poka, Parsable, SafetyCulture, MaintainX): inspections, checklists, work orders, adoption, pricing

### Takeaway
The category is mature and priced per user per month with free entry tiers (MaintainX, SafetyCulture free; paid roughly $16-75/user/month), while AI-heavy platforms (Augmentir, Poka) are quote-only enterprise sales. Reported effects cluster around 30-57% faster onboarding/inspection and 10-20% productivity gains, all vendor-reported.

### Inferences
- Commercial pattern: free/cheap self-serve tier to get the floor using it (SafetyCulture, MaintainX), then upsell per seat. AI-first platforms (Augmentir, Poka/IFS) sell top-down to multi-plant enterprises with no public pricing.
- The value story vendors sell is training/onboarding time and "tribal knowledge" capture (QR-code instructions, micro-lessons, skills matrix), not just checklists.
- Proposed catalogue of 10 solution types for the final report (each detailed in the sections below): (1) connected-worker checklists/inspections; (2) CMMS/work orders mobile; (3) skills/microlearning and digital work instructions; (4) AI video EHS (PPE, vehicle-pedestrian zones); (5) digital permit-to-work/control of work; (6) voice/AI shift handover and logbooks; (7) offline field ticketing and crew time tracking; (8) fleet/heavy equipment telematics with AI dashcams and fuel fraud controls; (9) mining shift reporting and AI drill & blast; (10) grain elevator scale/farmer portal and ready-mix dispatch.

### Gaps
- No public pricing found for Augmentir, Poka, or Parsable. Parsable-specific 2024-2026 outcome numbers were not found in this pass.
- Most Poka studies appear to describe 2018-2020 rollouts; publication years 2024-2026 could not be confirmed.
- No independent audit of any of these outcome numbers.

- Permit-to-work digitization is sold to oil & gas and mining majors; the main measurable effect is time to issue permits and audit effort, not injury rates.

- No quantified 2024-2026 case for AI-generated incident reports or AI-authored training content.

## 3. Voice/AI shift reports and handovers, offline-first field data capture (field ticketing), crew/rotation scheduling

### Takeaway
Digital field ticketing in oilfield services is the most proven category (operators force suppliers onto networks such as Enverus OpenInvoice/OpenTicket; ticket approval times halved, DSO cut from 60-90 to 30-60 days). AI voice shift handover is an emerging 2025-2026 category with only vendor claims. Crew time tracking is cheap ($0-15/user/month).

## 6. Adoption barriers reported (connectivity, worker resistance, cost)

### Takeaway
The best 2025 survey data (Weavix, vendor-run, 300 US manufacturing workers) says workers are mostly open to AI (74%); the bigger barriers are device policy (64% face smartphone restrictions on the floor) and legacy radios (67%). Cost barriers show up as per-user/per-vehicle pricing complaints; connectivity drives offline-first design.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- For CIS/Kazakhstan transfer: phone bans on production floors and in hazardous zones (ATEX/explosion-proof requirements in oil & gas and mining) mean device choice (rugged/intrinsically safe devices, kiosk tablets, voice via radio) is a real deployment cost, beyond software license.
- Hub-mandated adoption (operator, elevator) overcomes supplier resistance better than voluntary bottom-up adoption.
